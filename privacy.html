Speedy jumper Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 14, 2026

Overview

Speedy jumper is a mobile game. You can play without creating an account. This policy explains how the game and its service providers process information.

Information Stored on Your Device

The game stores gameplay information on your device. This information can include your best score, best streak, coins, daily progress, settings, and a randomly generated player identifier. You can remove this information by deleting the app.

Advertising and Consent

Speedy jumper uses Google AdMob to display interstitial and rewarded ads. Google User Messaging Platform is used to request and manage advertising consent where required. Depending on your consent choices and device settings, Google and its advertising partners may process information such as:

  • IP address and approximate location derived from the IP address.
  • Device and advertising identifiers, when permitted.
  • Ad views, clicks, rewards, and other interactions with the app.
  • Diagnostic, performance, and fraud-prevention information.

Ads can be personalized or non-personalized according to your consent choices, location, and device settings. You do not need to allow tracking to play the game. You can manage tracking permissions in iOS Settings.

Learn more in the Google Privacy Policy and Google's partner data information.

Online Services and Hosting

When online services are used, the game can send realtime gameplay messages such as movement, shots, damage, enemy events, and connection status. The server and hosting provider can also process IP addresses and basic connection logs needed for security and service operation. The public website is hosted through Cloudflare.

Learn more in the Cloudflare Privacy Policy.

Information the Game Does Not Request

The game does not require your name, email address, contacts, camera, microphone, photos, or precise location. Support emails are processed only when you choose to contact us.

Data Retention

Local gameplay information remains on your device until you delete the app or its data. Google, Cloudflare, Apple, and other service providers retain information according to their own policies and legal requirements.

Children

Speedy jumper is not specifically directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly ask children to provide personal information directly to us.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy when the game, its advertising features, or its service providers change. The latest version is published on this page.
