Speedy jumper Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 12, 2026

Speedy jumper does not require an account. The game stores local gameplay records on your device, such as best score, best streak, coins, daily progress, and a random player identifier used to keep online player slots stable during a session.

When online play is used, the game server receives realtime gameplay messages needed to run the match, such as movement, shots, damage, enemy events, and connection status. These messages are used to operate gameplay and are not intended to personally identify players.

Speedy jumper does not include third-party advertising or analytics SDKs in this build.
