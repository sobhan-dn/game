Void Spheres Duel

Two-minute sphere duel

Score for every red unit you destroy and every online rival hit you land. When the clock hits zero, the higher score wins.

  • Online First browser is Player 1, second browser is Player 2
  • W A S D Move across the spheres
  • Space / Shift Jump between spheres
  • Click / Enter Fire through the crosshair
  • Mouse Aim the camera
  • Touch iPhone: left stick moves, right side aims, buttons jump/fire
  • Timer 2 minutes, highest score wins

For best aim, tap the playfield once after starting.
