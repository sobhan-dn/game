Speedy jumper can be played without an account.

This build was updated after a Guideline 4.3(a) review message. It is not a reskin of the earlier project: the submitted game has its own Speedy jumper identity, 3D spherical arena traversal, two-minute scoring rules, red-unit pressure, persistent streak/coin/daily progress, and a Rift Shard / Rift Surge mechanic. Reviewers can see the mechanic in Solo vs AI by collecting the floating teal shards until the Rift meter reaches 100%, then tapping SURGE.

To test:
1. Launch the app.
2. Select Solo vs AI.
3. Tap Start Match.
4. Use the left touch stick to move, the Jump button to jump, and the Fire button to shoot.
5. Collect four teal Rift Shards, then tap SURGE to trigger the area burst.
6. Score points to see coins, streaks, best score, and daily target progress update.

Online Duel requires two separate clients. If no second player is available, use Solo vs AI for immediate gameplay review.
