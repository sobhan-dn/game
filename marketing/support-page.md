# Speedy jumper Support

For support with Speedy jumper, contact the developer through the public support channel listed on the App Store page.

## Common Questions

### The game feels hard

Use movement constantly, jump between spheres, and aim for streak bonuses by chaining red-unit kills and direct hits.

### The app has no sound

Sound starts after the first tap because mobile browsers and iOS apps require user interaction before audio can play.
