# Void Spheres: Riftbound Support

For support with Void Spheres: Riftbound, use the public support page published with the game site or contact the developer through the App Store product page. Core gameplay is offline-ready and single-player; the native iOS build uses network access for post-match advertising and privacy messages when available.

## Common Questions

### The game feels hard

Use movement constantly, jump between spheres, collect teal Rift Shards, and aim for streak bonuses by chaining red-unit kills and direct hits.

### The app has no sound

Sound starts after the first tap because mobile browsers and iOS apps require user interaction before audio can play.

## App Store Review

The Solo vs AI match starts without an account or internet connection and lets reviewers verify movement, shooting, Rift Shards, Surge, coins, streaks, and daily progress.
