# Privacy Policy for Void Spheres: Riftbound

Last updated: August 16, 2026

This policy covers the Void Spheres: Riftbound mobile game. Core gameplay is single-player, works without an account, and can continue when the device is offline. The native iOS app uses network access for advertising and privacy messages when those services are available.

## Data We Store on Your Device

The game may save gameplay preferences and records locally on your device, including your best score, best streak, coins, daily progress, and Rift progress. This information is not sent to the developer. You can remove it by deleting the app, subject to your device's normal backup and restore behavior.

## Advertising and Consent

The native iOS app uses Google AdMob to display a standard interstitial advertisement between completed matches. Google User Messaging Platform is used to request and manage advertising choices where required. Depending on your location, consent choices, device settings, and the ad served, Google and its advertising partners may process information such as:

- IP address and approximate location derived from the IP address.
- Device and advertising identifiers when permitted by the operating system and your choices.
- Ad views, clicks, and other interactions with an advertisement.
- Diagnostic, performance, security, and fraud-prevention information.

The developer does not sell personal data or operate an account, multiplayer, analytics, or cloud-synchronization service. Ad availability does not determine access to saved gameplay progress: if an ad cannot be requested or shown, replay is unlocked without it. The browser preview does not initialize AdMob.

For more information, see the [Google Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy) and [How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services](https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites).

## Device Permissions

The game does not request access to your contacts, camera, microphone, precise location, or photo library. Advertising choices can be reviewed from the in-app **Ad Privacy** entry point when Google requires that option.

## Children's Privacy

The game is not directed to children under 13, and the developer does not knowingly collect personal information from children. Store age ratings and the AdMob account's audience settings must be configured consistently before release.

## Data Retention

Local gameplay records remain on your device until you clear the app's data or delete the app, subject to normal device backup behavior. Google and its advertising partners retain information according to their own policies and legal obligations.

## Changes to This Policy

If the game's data practices or advertising providers change, this policy will be updated before the affected version is released.
