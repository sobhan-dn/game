Void Spheres: Riftbound Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 16, 2026

This policy covers the Void Spheres: Riftbound mobile game. Core gameplay is single-player, works without an account, and can continue when the device is offline. The native iOS app uses network access for advertising and privacy messages when available.

Data Stored on Your Device

The game may save gameplay preferences and records locally on your device, including your best score, best streak, coins, daily progress, and Rift progress. This information is not sent to the developer. You can remove it by deleting the app, subject to your device's normal backup and restore behavior.

Advertising and Consent

The native iOS app uses Google AdMob to display a standard interstitial advertisement between completed matches. Google User Messaging Platform requests and manages advertising choices where required. Depending on your location, choices, device settings, and the ad served, Google and its advertising partners may process:

  • IP address and approximate location derived from the IP address.
  • Device and advertising identifiers when permitted.
  • Ad views, clicks, and other interactions with an advertisement.
  • Diagnostic, performance, security, and fraud-prevention information.

The developer does not sell personal data or operate an account, multiplayer, analytics, or cloud-synchronization service. If an ad is unavailable, replay is unlocked without it. The browser preview does not initialize AdMob.

Learn more in the Google Privacy Policy and Google's partner data information.

Device Permissions

The game does not request access to your contacts, camera, microphone, precise location, or photo library. An in-app Ad Privacy option is shown when Google requires it.

Children's Privacy

The game is not directed to children under 13, and the developer does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Data Retention

Local gameplay records remain on your device until you clear the app's data or delete the app. Google and its advertising partners retain information under their own policies and legal obligations.

Changes to This Policy

If the game's data practices or advertising providers change, this policy will be updated before the affected version is released.
