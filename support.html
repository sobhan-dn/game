Frequently Asked Questions

How do I play?

Select Solo vs AI, tap Start Match, move with the left stick, aim by dragging on the right side, and use Jump, Fire, and Surge during the match. Collect four teal Rift Shards to fill the Rift meter and activate Surge.

Can I play without an internet connection?

Yes. Core Solo vs AI gameplay and saved progress work without an account or connection. The native iOS app uses the network for post-match ads and privacy messages; if an ad is unavailable, replay unlocks automatically.

How do I report an inappropriate or broken ad?

Use the ad's own close or report control when available, then email support with the time, your country, and a screenshot if possible. Do not include sensitive personal information.

The game is not opening. What should I try?

Restart the app, make sure your device has enough free storage, and check that you are using the latest version from the App Store.

How do I report a problem?

Please email us with a short description of the issue, your device model, and the iOS version you are using.
