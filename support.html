Frequently Asked Questions

How do I play?

Tap Start Match, move with the left stick, aim by dragging on the right side, and use Jump, Fire, and Surge during the two-minute match. Collect four teal Rift Shards to fill the Rift meter and activate Surge. The player with the highest score wins.

Why does an ad appear before a match?

The game can show an interstitial ad before a match. A notice appears first, and the match timer starts only after the ad closes.

How do rewarded ads work?

After a completed match, you can choose to watch a rewarded ad to double the coins earned in that match. The reward is optional and is granted only after the ad completes.

Can I change my advertising privacy choices?

Where required, the game displays a Google consent form before requesting ads. You can also manage tracking permissions in the iOS Settings app.

The game is not opening. What should I try?

Restart the app, make sure your device has enough free storage, and check that you are using the latest version from the App Store.

How do I report a problem?

Please email us with a short description of the issue, your device model, and the iOS version you are using.
