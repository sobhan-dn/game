Speedy jumper is a fast 3D arena battler built for quick mobile matches.

Jump across floating neon spheres, collect Rift Shards, aim through the crosshair, fire at your rival, and survive the pressure from red enemy units. Every match lasts two minutes, so every hit, dodge, surge burst, streak, and coin reward matters.

Play instantly in Solo vs AI, or switch to Online Duel and fight another player in real time. The controls are simple to learn on iPhone: use the left stick to move, drag to aim, then jump and fire with quick touch buttons.
